# Cost-Benefit Analysis of Residential Solar Adoption in the MMR

This repository contains the data, financial models, and final thesis for a field study assessing the market barriers associated with residential rooftop solar (RTS) adoption in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR). 

This research evaluates the financial architecture of the PM Surya Ghar scheme against structural and behavioral market frictions, completed as part of the M.A. Economics program at the University of Mumbai.

## Methodological Framework & Key Outputs
* **Financial Modeling:** Constructed a 25-year Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) model demonstrating a sub-3-year discounted payback period and a baseline NPV of ₹1.96 Lakhs for an optimized 2 kW system. 
* **Sensitivity Testing:** Applied one-way sensitivity analysis to the discount rate (6% to 10%) to test capital viability and project resilience against macroeconomic interest rate shocks.
* **Barrier Analysis:** Synthesized primary survey data to quantify adoption bottlenecks, identifying upfront capital risk perception (38%), Cooperative Housing Society (CHS) structural limits (61.5%), and localized information asymmetry as primary constraints.
* **Tariff Reconciliation:** Adjusted baseline assumptions to account for the MMR composite tariff premium, reconciling local DCF outputs with broader national literature estimates.

## Repository Structure
* `Cost-Benefit_Analysis_Solar_MMR.pdf` - The complete research paper and methodology.
* `DCF_Sensitivity_Model.xlsx` - The financial model containing the Discounted Cash Flow math, Payback Period tracking, and Sensitivity Analysis. 
* `survey_responses_raw.csv` - The anonymized primary data collected from MMR households.

## Conclusion
The analysis highlights the gap between macro-level subsidy deployment and micro-level consumer psychology, reinforcing the need for targeted financial structuring and institutional coordination in the distributed energy sector. This work builds on ongoing research interests in energy economics, infrastructure capital allocation, and climate finance.
